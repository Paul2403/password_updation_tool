Why do you need a strong password?

A strong password is necessary to enhance the security of your online accounts and protect your personal information. Strong passwords are often lengthy, complicated, and unique, making them challenging for automated hacking techniques. You may lessen the chance that unauthorized people can access your critical information and preserve your online privacy and security by using a secure password.

What is MFA? What is its importance?

MFA, or Multi-Factor Authentication, is a security measure that requires users to provide multiple verification factors to prove their identity when accessing online accounts. Its importance lies in significantly enhancing account security by adding an extra layer of protection beyond passwords. MFA reduces the risk of unauthorized access, prevents attacks like password guessing or credential theft, and provides peace of mind knowing that sensitive information is better safeguarded.
